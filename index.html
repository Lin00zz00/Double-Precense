Double Presence 双重在场

非对称双人MR体验 Asymmetric Two-Person MR Experience

本作品以沉浸式戏剧与混合现实（MR）技术为核心，设计构建了一套双重视域的MR体验。系统通过VR与MR模式的动态切换，以及“基于他者的锚定”机制，使参与者在空间翻转、信息错位与非对称叙事中共同推动剧情发展。双方参与者既互为彼此的空间锚点，也成为对方叙事中的一部分。作品以作者个人经历为灵感，探讨AI时代下身份盗用、信息操控与数字替身等社会议题，回应公众对于真实性、隐私与身份边界的焦虑。 This work centers on immersive theater and mixed reality (MR), constructing a dual-perspective MR experience. Through dynamic switching between VR and MR modes and an "other-anchored" mechanism, participants co-advance the plot amid spatial inversion, information misalignment, and asymmetric storytelling. Each participant is both the other's spatial anchor and part of the other's narrative. Inspired by the author's own experience, the project examines identity theft, information manipulation, and digital doubles in the AI era, responding to public anxiety around authenticity, privacy, and identity boundaries.

故事概要 Story Summary

2036年，一起主播坠楼案件引发舆论争议，嫌疑人B被指控利用AI深度伪造技术制造虚假影像。证人A作为技术专家进入“案件时空重建程序”，通过虚拟回溯调查案发现场。然而，系统在重建过程中不断篡改、补全与拼接证据，使两位参与者逐渐陷入对彼此与“真实”的怀疑之中。 In 2036, a streamer’s fatal fall sparks public controversy. Suspect B is accused of fabricating false footage using AI deepfake technology. Witness A, a technical expert, enters a “spatiotemporal case reconstruction program” to investigate through virtual replay. Yet the system keeps altering, completing, and stitching evidence, drawing both participants into growing suspicion of each other and of reality itself.
